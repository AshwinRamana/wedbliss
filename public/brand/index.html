Typography & usage

Fraunces for editorial headings. DM Sans for interface copy. The downloadable wordmark uses a Georgia serif fallback; the website uses its loaded brand font.

Minimum symbol size: 24px. Leave at least one quarter of its width clear on each side. Use wine on paper or ivory on wine. Never stretch the symbol, add shadows, or combine it with ornamental illustrations.

Voice

Warm, direct and considered. Short sentences. Clear actions. Make space for the couple’s story.
